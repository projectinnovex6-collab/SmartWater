Detect pipe leaks early. Isolate the section. Alert on mobile.

A single-branch smart water model that watches pressure at two points (S1 → S2). When the difference stays above 15 kPa for 3 seconds, the system confirms a leak, closes the solenoid valve, and can notify the operator via ESP32.

How the Arduino decides

Same steps as the live demo and the physical prototype.

Read S1 and S2

Average 20 ADC samples and convert to kPa.

Compute difference

ΔP = S1 − S2. A healthy short pipe only loses a few kPa.

Confirm for 3 seconds

If ΔP stays above 15 kPa for 3 s, the leak is confirmed.

Isolate and alert

Alarm + close the NC solenoid valve. Optional ESP32 mobile message.

On stage the leak is created with a ball valve (leak tap) between S1 and S2 — the pipe is never cut.

The life of a leak, step by step

Scroll down and watch the line fill as the story moves along.

  1. t = 0 s

    A leak opens between S1 and S2.

  2. A moment later

    Water escapes, so the pressure at S2 falls while S1 stays about the same.

  3. ΔP crosses 15 kPa

    S1 − S2 rises above the limit and the 3-second timer starts.

  4. t + 3 s

    ΔP is still high, so the leak is confirmed. It was not just a spike.

  5. Response

    Red LED and buzzer switch on. The relay cuts power and the valve closes.

  6. Afterwards

    Data goes to the laptop. The alarm stays on until someone checks the pipe and presses reset.

Questions people ask

Short answers. Drip can answer too: tap the droplet and choose Ask Drip.

Why 15 kPa?

A healthy short pipe only loses a few kPa between S1 and S2. 15 kPa sits clearly above that normal loss, so it trips only when something is really wrong.

Why wait 3 seconds?

Short pump spikes pass quickly. The 3-second window ignores them, so the valve is closed only for a real leak.

Do you cut the pipe to make a leak?

No. A ball valve, the leak tap, sits between S1 and S2. Opening it creates the leak and closing it fixes it.

What does the ESP32 do?

It sends a mobile warning to a saved operator number when a leak is confirmed. The demo shows a Telegram-style alert.

What happens after the valve closes?

The alarm is on and that section is isolated. In the demo, press Repair & reset to start over.

Can I try it without the hardware?

Yes. The interactive demo runs in your browser on a phone, tablet or laptop.

Is the demo real hardware?

No. The web demo is a dummy simulation. The readings, alarm, valve and phone alert are simulated, nothing is connected to real hardware, and the model has no LCD display.

Why use a normally-closed valve?

It shuts by itself when it loses power. We feed it through the relay, so cutting that power on a leak closes it and stops the loss.

What does P = ρgh mean?

Pressure from a liquid column equals its density (ρ) times the acceleration due to gravity (g) times its height (h). For water that is about 9.8 kPa per metre.
